Speed, precision and round-the-clock access are rewriting the rules of options trading, according to a panel of industry leaders who traced the industry's transformation from paper trading floors to pushes for an always-on, potentially AI-assisted marketplace. At Robinhood’s annual HOOD Summit in Houston, Cboe’s Henry Schwartz and Rob Hocking joined Robinhood’s Steve Quirk for a conversation about the evolution of options trading, diving deep into the increase in expirations and same-day trading.
